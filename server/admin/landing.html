End-to-end, post-quantum

Hybrid ML-KEM-1024 and P-384 key agreement with a Double Ratchet, so every message has its own key and old keys are gone.

Blind relay

Sealed sender and rotating routing tags. The relay stores an opaque blob under a tag it cannot link to anyone, and deletes it on delivery.

Clean media

Photos and files are stripped of location, camera and author metadata before they are encrypted, and expire after a day.

The rules this relay is built around

  1. It never shuts down. Anyone can run a relay, including over Tor.
  2. It cannot tell who sent a message. The sender is inside the encrypted envelope.
  3. It cannot tell who a message is for. Mail is filed under rotating tags and deleted when collected.
  4. Nothing you send carries identifying metadata. Every media path is scrubbed before encryption.
